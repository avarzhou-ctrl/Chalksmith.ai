What is Elapsed Time?

Elapsed Time is just a fancy math word for: "How much time has passed?" from when you start an activity to when you finish!

1. Start Time

The exact moment your adventure begins.

Example: 2:15 PM
(Recess starts! 🏃‍♂️)

2. Elapsed Time

The middle part. How long the activity actually lasts.

"How long did we play?"

3. End Time

The exact moment the game is over.

Example: 2:45 PM
(Back to class! 📝)

The Secret Clock Rule: Base-60!

In regular school math, we count by 10s. But clocks are different! They use a super secret rule called Base-60.

  • 1 Hour = 60 Minutes
  • There is no such thing as "100 minutes" on a clock!
  • When we trade hours for minutes, we never trade for 10. We always trade for 60 minutes!

Watch Out for the Trap! ⚠️

The Subtraction Mistake:

  2:10
- 1:40
-------
  0:70? ❌ No way!

Clocks don't have 70 minutes!

Method 2: The T-Chart Trick

The T-Chart trick is like keeping a scoreboard. It keeps all your numbers organized!

Write your target times on the left side, and write down how big your jumps are on the right side. Then add up the right side!

Method 3: The Trading Trick!

Sometimes you can subtract to find the answer. But if the top minutes are smaller, you have to trade 1 hour for 60 minutes!

How to Trade Time:

Subtract: 4:15 - 1:45
(You can't do 15 - 45!)

1. Take 1 Hour from the 4.
   4 Hours → 3 Hours
2. Turn that hour into 60 minutes and add it to your 15.
   15 + 60 = 75 Minutes

New Math Equation:
   3:75 - 1:45 = 2:30

It looks like this on paper:

   3 hours   75 mins
-  1 hour    45 mins
--------------------
   2 hours   30 mins

Just remember: 1 hour is exactly equal to 60 minutes. You are just trading!

Crossing AM to PM

Things can get confusing when you jump past 12:00 PM (Noon). That's when morning turns to afternoon!

🍔 The "Noon Bridge" Strategy

Use 12:00 (lunchtime!) as a bridge. Jump to 12:00 first, then jump the rest of the way.

Example: 10:30 AM to 2:15 PM

  • 10:30 AM → 12:00 PM = 1 hr 30 mins
  • 12:00 PM → 2:15 PM = 2 hrs 15 mins
  • Add them: 3 hours and 45 minutes!
