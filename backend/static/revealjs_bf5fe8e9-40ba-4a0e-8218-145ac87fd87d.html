Understanding Area

The Geometry of Two-Dimensional Space

What is Area?

  • Definition: Area is the quantity that expresses the extent of a two-dimensional figure or shape in a plane.
  • Unlike perimeter (which measures distance around), area measures the surface covered.
  • Standard units: Square units (e.g., cm², m², in²).

Rectangles & Squares

To find the area of a rectangle, multiply the length by the width.

Area = l × w

Note: For a square, since all sides are equal ($s$), the area is $s^2$.

Triangles

A triangle is essentially half of a parallelogram with the same base and height.

Area = ½ × b × h
  • Base (b): Any side of the triangle.
  • Height (h): The perpendicular distance from the base to the opposite vertex.

Circles

The area of a circle depends on its radius ($r$).

Area = πr²
  • Radius ($r$): Distance from the center to the edge.
  • Pi ($\pi$): A constant approximately equal to 3.14159.

Composite Shapes

To find the area of complex shapes, decompose them into simpler shapes you already know.

Total Area = Area 1 + Area 2

Key Terminology

Dimension
A measurable extent of some kind, such as length, breadth, depth, or height.
Perpendicular
A relationship between two lines which meet at a right angle (90 degrees).
Plane
A flat, two-dimensional surface that extends infinitely far.

Formula Summary

Shape Variable Definitions Area Formula
Rectangle $l$ = length, $w$ = width $l \times w$
Triangle $b$ = base, $h$ = height $\frac{1}{2}bh$
Circle $r$ = radius, $\pi \approx 3.14$ $\pi r^2$
Square $s$ = side length $s^2$
