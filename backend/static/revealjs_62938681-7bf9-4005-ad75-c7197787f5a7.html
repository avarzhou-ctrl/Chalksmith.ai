Understanding Area

Measuring Two-Dimensional Space

Mathematics Department

What is Area?

  • Area is the amount of space inside the boundary of a flat (2D) object.
  • While perimeter measures the "fence" around a shape, area measures the "grass" inside.
  • Area is always measured in square units (e.g., cm², m², in²).

Area of a Rectangle

To find the area, multiply the base by the height.

Area = b × h

Note: Often expressed as Length × Width.

Special Case: The Square

Since all sides are equal in a square ($s$), the formula simplifies:

Area = s × s = s²

Area of a Triangle

A triangle is exactly half of a rectangle with the same base and height.

Area = ½ × b × h

Area of a Circle

Requires the radius (r) and the constant π (approx. 3.14159).

Area = πr²

Definition: Radius is the distance from the center to the edge.

Formula Summary

Shape Formula Key Variables
Rectangle $b \times h$ Base, Height
Square $s^2$ Side
Triangle $\frac{1}{2}bh$ Base, Vertical Height
Circle $\pi r^2$ Radius

Quick Check!

What is the area of a rectangle with a base of 8cm and a height of 5cm?

Calculation: $8 \text{ cm} \times 5 \text{ cm}$

Result: 40 cm²

⚠️ Remember: Units must be squared!

Key Takeaways

  1. Area is 2D space measurement.
  2. Standard units are always squared ($u^2$).
  3. Complex shapes can be broken down into simpler rectangles and triangles to find total area.
