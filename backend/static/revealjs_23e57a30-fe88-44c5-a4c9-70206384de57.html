What is Elapsed Time?

Elapsed Time is the exact amount of time that passes from the start of an event to the end of that event.

1. Start Time

The specific moment an activity begins.

Example: 2:15 PM

2. Elapsed Time

The duration or length of the activity.

The "How Long?"

3. End Time

The exact moment the activity finishes.

Example: 4:45 PM

The Gold Standard: Base-60

Unlike normal math which uses base-10 groups, time calculations are built on a Base-60 system.

  • 1 Hour = 60 Minutes
  • When subtracting or regrouping, you never borrow "10". You must borrow 60 minutes.
  • Going past the 60-minute mark rolls the hour dial over to the next unit.

Watch Out for the Trap!

Incorrect Subtraction Trap:

  2:10
- 1:40
-------
  0:70? ❌ (No! Time is not base-10!)

Method 3: Direct Subtraction

Subtracting works when the minutes of the end time are greater than the start time. If they aren't, you must regroup!

The Regrouping Formula

Subtract: 4:15 - 1:45

1. Borrow 1 Hour from 4.
   4 Hours → 3 Hours
2. Add 60 minutes to 15.
   15 Mins → 75 Minutes

New Equation:
   3:75 - 1:45 = 2:30

Let's verify the math side-by-side:

   3 hours   75 mins
-  1 hour    45 mins
--------------------
   2 hours   30 mins

Always make sure to convert back once done if minutes exceed 60.

Crossing the AM to PM Boundary

Calculations can get confusing when transitioning across 12:00 PM (Noon) or 12:00 AM (Midnight).

💡 The "Bridge to 12" Strategy

Use 12:00 as a central bridge checkpoint. Calculate time up to 12:00, then add the rest.

Example: 10:30 AM to 2:15 PM

  • 10:30 AM → 12:00 PM = 1 hr 30 mins
  • 12:00 PM → 2:15 PM = 2 hrs 15 mins
  • Total: 3 hours and 45 minutes

Practice Challenges

Test your knowledge! Can you solve these real-world scenarios?

Challenge #1

The Movie Problem

A movie starts at 7:15 PM and is 2 hours and 25 minutes long. What time does it end?

Answer: 9:40 PM
(7:15 + 2 hours = 9:15. Plus 25 mins = 9:40)
Challenge #2

The Flight Duration

A flight takes off at 11:45 AM and lands at 3:10 PM. How long was the flight?

Answer: 3 Hours & 25 Mins
(Bridge: +15m to 12:00, then +3h 10m to 3:10)

Keys to Success!

Keep these rules in mind whenever you calculate elapsed time:

Remember Base-60

Never regroup in tens. 1 Hour is always replaced by, or converted to, 60 Minutes.

Bridge the Noon

When transitioning through AM/PM, use 12:00 as a resting spot to avoid offset errors.

Draw Timelines

Visual representation using mountains (hours) and hills (minutes) decreases arithmetic slips.
