Understanding Area

Measuring Two-Dimensional Space

What is Area?

  • Area is the amount of 2D space inside a boundary.
  • While Perimeter measures the distance around a shape, Area measures the space within it.
  • Area is always measured in square units (e.g., cm², m², in²).

Rectangles & Squares

The area is calculated by multiplying the base by the height.

Area = base × height

Note: In a square, all sides are equal, so Area = side².

Area of a Triangle

A triangle is exactly half of a rectangle with the same base and height.

Area = ½ × base × height

Area of a Circle

For circles, we use the constant π (Pi), which is approximately 3.14159.

Area = π × r²

Formula Summary

Shape Formula Variables
Rectangle $b \times h$ Base, Height
Square $s^2$ Side
Triangle $\frac{1}{2}bh$ Base, Height
Circle $\pi r^2$ Radius

Glossary of Terms

2D (Two-Dimensional)
A flat shape having length and width, but no depth.
Pi (π)
A mathematical constant (~3.14) representing the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter.
Radius
The distance from the center of a circle to its edge.
Square Unit
A square with sides of length 1, used as a standard measure of area.

Quick Quiz

  1. If a rectangle has a base of 5cm and a height of 4cm, what is its area? Answer: 20cm²
  2. Why is the formula for a triangle multiplied by ½? Answer: Because a triangle is half of a rectangle/parallelogram.
