Area of Polygons

Parallelograms and Triangles

Geometry Foundations: Understanding Space and Dimension

Essential Vocabulary

  • Base (b): Any side of a parallelogram or triangle.
  • Height (h): The perpendicular distance from the base to the opposite side or vertex.
  • Perpendicular: Meeting at a 90-degree (right) angle.
  • Area: The amount of 2D space inside a shape, measured in square units (e.g., cm², in²).

Area of a Parallelogram

A parallelogram can be transformed into a rectangle by moving a right-triangle section from one side to the other.

Area = base × height
$A = b \cdot h$

The "Height" Trap

Always use the perpendicular height, not the slanted side length!

Area of a Triangle

Every triangle is exactly half of a parallelogram with the same base and height.

Area = ½ × base × height
$A = \frac{1}{2}bh$

Practice 1: Parallelogram

Find the area of the parallelogram:

  • Base = 12 cm
  • Height = 7 cm
  • Slant side = 8 cm

Solution:

$A = b \cdot h$
$A = 12 \cdot 7$
$A = 84 \text{ cm}^2$

*Note: We ignore the 8 cm slant side.

Practice 2: Triangle

Find the area of the triangle:

  • Base = 10 m
  • Height = 6 m

Solution:

$A = \frac{1}{2}bh$
$A = \frac{1}{2}(10 \cdot 6)$
$A = \frac{1}{2}(60)$
$A = 30 \text{ m}^2$

Quick Reference

Shape Formula Key Relationship
Parallelogram $A = bh$ Same as a rectangle
Triangle $A = \frac{1}{2}bh$ Half of a parallelogram

Pro Tip: Always look for the square symbol (∟) to identify the height!
