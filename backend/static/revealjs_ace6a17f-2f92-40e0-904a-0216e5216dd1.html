What is Elapsed Time?

Elapsed time is the measure of how much time passes from the start of an event to the end of that event.

Start Time

The moment an event begins.

Example: 8:30 AM

End Time

The moment an event finishes.

Example: 10:15 AM

The Base-60 Warning

Why can't we just use standard column subtraction?

Common Fatal Error:

10:15 - 8:30 is NOT calculated as "10.15 - 8.30 = 1.85"

Standard subtraction uses Base-10 (units of 100).
Time tracking uses Base-60 (1 Hour = 60 Minutes).

To calculate elapsed time accurately, we must use visual frameworks or regroup hours into 60 minutes.

Strategy 1: The Linear Timeline

Great for mapping jumps to the next hour marker.

Total Time = 1 hour + 30 min + 15 min = 1 hr 45 min

Strategy 2: The T-Chart Method

Excellent for accounting long durations step-by-step.

Time Counter Amount Added
8:30 AM Start
9:30 AM + 1 hour
10:00 AM + 30 min
10:15 AM + 15 min

Rule: Add hours first to get close to the destination, then adjust the remaining minutes.

Summing it up:

1 hr + 30 min + 15 min = 1 hour 45 minutes

Practice 1: Within Day boundaries

Calculate the elapsed time from 3:15 PM to 7:40 PM.

Step 1: Leap forward by full hours without overshoot.

3:15 PM → 7:15 PM = 4 hours

Step 2: Compute remaining minutes to reach target.

7:15 PM → 7:40 PM = 25 minutes (since 40 - 15 = 25)

Total Elapsed Time: 4 hours 25 minutes

Practice 2: Crossing AM to PM

An event starts at 10:45 AM and concludes at 2:15 PM.

Anchor Strategy: Use 12:00 PM (Noon) as your anchor.

Segment A: Morning run to Noon

10:45 AM → 12:00 PM = 1 hour 15 minutes

Segment B: Afternoon run from Noon

12:00 PM → 2:15 PM = 2 hours 15 minutes

Combine segments: 1hr 15m + 2hr 15m

Result: 3 hours 30 minutes

The Regrouping Math Strategy

For algebraic calculations, borrow 1 Hour (60 mins) when subtraction is blocked.

Problem:

  5:15 PM
- 2:45 PM

Since 15 is smaller than 45, standard column subtraction fails.

Regroup:

Borrow 1 hour from the 5 hours (leaving 4). Add 60 minutes to the 15 minutes.

New Math Setup:
  4:75
- 2:45
------
  2:30 → 2h 30m

Key Takeaways for Success

  • 1
    Watch the 12-Hour Shift: When crossing AM to PM, remember that Noon (12:00 PM) marks the transition point.
  • 2
    Choose Your Strategy: Use Number Lines for quick mental visual jumps, and T-Charts to log complex segments.
  • 3
    Convert Carefully: Always maintain the rule: 1 Hour = 60 Minutes. Never treat time digits as decimals!
