Understanding Area

Measuring the Space Within Boundaries

Mathematics Fundamentals

What is Area?

Definition: Area is the quantity that expresses the extent of a two-dimensional region, shape, or planar lamina in the plane.

  • It measures the surface of an object.
  • Area is measured in square units (e.g., cm², m², in²).

Visualizing Square Units

We measure area by counting how many unit squares fit inside.

Area = Length × Width

Rectangles & Squares

A = l × w

Where:
l = Length
w = Width

Area of a Triangle

A triangle is exactly half of a rectangle with the same base and height.

A = ½ × b × h

b = Base
h = Vertical Height

Area of a Circle

Based on the radius (distance from center to edge).

A = πr²

π (Pi) ≈ 3.14159...
r = Radius

Quick Reference

Shape Formula Key Variables
Square s² s = side
Rectangle l × w l = length, w = width
Triangle ½bh b = base, h = height
Circle πr² r = radius

Summary

  • Area is 2D space.
  • Always use squared units in your answer.
  • Complex shapes can be broken down into simpler rectangles and triangles to find the total area.
