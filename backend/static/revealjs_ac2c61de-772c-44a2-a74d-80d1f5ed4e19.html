Exploring Area

The Mathematics of 2D Space

What is Area?

In geometry, Area is the quantity that expresses the extent of a two-dimensional figure or shape in a plane.

Think of it as the amount of paint needed to cover a surface or the number of unit squares that fit inside a shape.

Measurement Units

Area is always measured in square units.

  • Square Millimeters (mm²)
  • Square Centimeters (cm²)
  • Square Meters (m²)
  • Square Kilometers (km²)
Example: A room size is 12 m²

Area of a Rectangle

To find the area of a rectangle, multiply the Length by the Width.

Area = l × w

Area of a Triangle

A triangle is exactly half of a rectangle with the same base and height.

Area = ½ × b × h

Area of a Circle

Calculated using the Radius (distance from center to edge) and the constant π (Pi ≈ 3.14159).

Area = π × r²

Key Terminology

2D Shape
A flat shape that has length and width but no thickness.

Perimeter
The distance around a shape (don't confuse with Area!)

Pi (π)
The ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter (approx. 3.14).

Dimension
A measurable extent, such as length, width, or height.

Quick Check

If a rectangle has a length of 5cm and a width of 4cm, what is its area?

5 × 4 = 20 cm²

Remember: Never forget the square units! (cm²)
