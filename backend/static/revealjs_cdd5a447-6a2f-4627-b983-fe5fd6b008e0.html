Mastering Area

Measuring the space within 2D boundaries

Mathematics Core Concepts

What is Area?

  • Area is the quantity that expresses the extent of a two-dimensional figure or shape in a plane.
  • Unlike perimeter (the distance around), area measures the surface coverage.
  • Measured in square units (e.g., cm², m², in²).

The Unit Square

We calculate area by counting how many unit squares fit inside a shape.

A unit square has a side length of 1.

Area of a Rectangle

Area = Length × Width
A = l × w

For a Square, since all sides are equal (s):

A = s²

Area of a Triangle

A triangle is exactly half of a rectangle with the same base and height.

Area = ½ × Base × Height
A = ½bh

Area of a Circle

The area of a circle depends on the radius (distance from center to edge).

Area = π × Radius²
A = πr²

Note: π (Pi) is approximately 3.14159

Glossary of Terms

2D (Two-Dimensional)
A flat shape with length and width, but no depth.

Square Units
The standard of measurement for area (e.g., a square with 1m sides = 1 m²).

Pi (π)
A mathematical constant representing the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter.
