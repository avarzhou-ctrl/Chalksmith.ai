Exploring Area

The Geometry of Surface Space

What is Area?

Definition: Area is the measurement of the total surface covered by a 2D shape or object.

  • It is a 2-dimensional quantity.
  • It is measured in square units (e.g., cm², m², in²).
  • Think of it as how many tiny squares are needed to cover a surface.

The Unit Square

Area is measured by counting Standard Units.

Area = Width × Height (for rectangles)

Rectangles & Squares

To find the area of a rectangle, multiply the base by the height.

Area = b × h

Triangles

A triangle is exactly half of a rectangle with the same base and height.

Area = ½ × b × h

Circles

Area calculation depends on the radius (the distance from the center to the edge).

Area = π × r²

(Where π ≈ 3.14159)

Key Terms Glossary

2D (Two-Dimensional): Having length and width, but no depth.
Base: The bottom boundary line of a geometric figure.
Height: The vertical distance from the base to the highest point.
Pi (π): A mathematical constant representing the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter.

Formula Summary

Shape Formula Variables
Rectangle $b \times h$ Base, Height
Triangle $\frac{1}{2} \times b \times h$ Base, Height
Circle $\pi \times r^2$ Radius
