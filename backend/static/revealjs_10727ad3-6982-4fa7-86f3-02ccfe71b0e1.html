Understanding Area

The Geometry of 2D Spaces

What is Area?

  • Area is the measurement of the total 2D space enclosed inside a boundary.
  • Unlike perimeter (which measures distance around), area measures the space inside.
  • It is strictly measured in square units (e.g., $u^2$, $cm^2$, $m^2$).

Rectangles & Squares

To find the area of a rectangle, we multiply the count of columns (length) by the count of rows (width).

Area = length × width
A = l × w

For a Square, since length equals width ($s$):

Area = side²
A = s²

Total Area = 3 × 2 = 6 units²

Triangles

Any triangle is exactly half of a parallelogram or rectangle with the same base and height.

Area = ½ × base × height
A = ½ × b × h
  • The height must be perpendicular (at a 90° angle) to the base.

Circles

The area of a circle depends on its radius (r)—the distance from the center to the edge.

Area = π × radius²
A = πr²
  • Pi (π) is a mathematical constant approximately equal to 3.14159.

Quick Reference Glossary

Shape Formula Key Terms
Rectangle A = l × w Length (l), Width (w)
Triangle A = ½ × b × h Base (b), perpendicular Height (h)
