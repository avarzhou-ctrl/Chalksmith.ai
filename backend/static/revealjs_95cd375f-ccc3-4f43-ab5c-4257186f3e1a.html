Defining the Formats

Fractions

Represents a ratio between a part and a whole using integers.

34

Numerator (parts) ÷ Denominator (total equal parts)

Decimals

Represents shares of a whole using base-10 positional notation.

0.75

Powers of ten determine position (tenths, hundredths...)

Percentages

An express division scale where the whole is standardized to exactly 100 parts.

75%

Literally translates to "per cent" (per hundred)

Visualizing Equivalence

Whether we write ¾, 0.75, or 75%, we represent the exact same proportion of a whole.

The Conversion Map

How to move seamlessly between representation systems:

Fractions ↔ Decimals

Fraction to Decimal
  1. Divide the numerator by the denominator.
  2. Add placeholders (.00) if performing long division.

Example: Convert 58
$5 \div 8 = $ 0.625

Decimal to Fraction
  1. Identify final place value position (e.g. hundredths).
  2. Write decimal digits as the numerator over that power of 10.
  3. Simplify the resulting fraction.

Example: Convert 0.35
$0.35 = $ 35100 $\rightarrow$ divide both by $5 \rightarrow$ 720

Decimals ↔ Percentages

The easiest conversion! This shift depends entirely on shifting the base-10 decimal point.
Decimal to Percent

Multiply by 100 (Shift the decimal point 2 places to the right) and append the % symbol.

0.125 → 12.5%
0.8 → 80.0% → 80%
Percent to Decimal

Divide by 100 (Shift the decimal point 2 places to the left) and drop the % symbol.

45% → 0.45
6% → 0.06

Real-World Applications

Financial Sales

Store markdowns are calculated in percentages (e.g., 40% off). If buying a $80 coat at 40% off, you pay:
$80 × 0.60 = $48

Engineering / Craft

Machinists and builders measure offsets using high-precision fractions or decimals.

"Drill a hole at 3/16 inch or 0.1875 inch depth."

Probability & Stats

Sports statistics and risk values rely on switching between systems.

A player batting 0.300 is successfully hitting a pitch 3 out of 10 times, or 30% of times.

Class Recap

💡 Fractions show the division relation of clean, individual integers.

💡 Decimals let us do fast computational arithmetic based on our base-10 numerical values.

💡 Percentages format any proportion into an easily digestible reference scale of 100.

Always remember: they represent different paths to the exact same value.
