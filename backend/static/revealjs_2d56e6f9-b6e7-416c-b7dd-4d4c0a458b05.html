Understanding Area

Measuring the Space Within

Created for Educational Excellence

What is Area?

  • Definition: The measurement of the total 2D surface contained within a specific boundary.
  • While perimeter measures the distance around the edge, area measures the space inside.
  • Area is always expressed in square units (e.g., cm², m², in²).

The Unit Square

We measure area by counting how many unit squares fit inside a shape.

Total Area = Sum of all unit squares

Rectangles

For a rectangle, we multiply the length by the width.

Area = Length × Width

If L=3 and W=3, Area = 9 square units.

Triangles

A triangle is exactly half of a rectangle with the same base and height.

Area = ½ × Base × Height

Circles

The area of a circle depends on its radius (r).

Area = π × r²

Where π (Pi) ≈ 3.14159

Composite Shapes

To find the area of complex shapes, decompose them into simpler parts.

Total Area = Area(A) + Area(B)

Key Terminology

Dimension
A measure in one direction (Length, Width, or Height).
Square Unit
A square with sides of 1 unit, used as the standard for area.
Pi (π)
The mathematical constant representing the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter.

Quick Review

✅ Area is 2D (flat).

✅ Units are always squared.

✅ Rectangles: $L \times W$

✅ Triangles: $\frac{1}{2} B \times H$

✅ Circles: $\pi r^2$
